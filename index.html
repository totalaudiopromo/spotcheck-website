Frequently Asked Questions

How does bot detection work?

SpotCheck evaluates mathematical ratios between follower counts, track updates, curator metadata, and historical growth patterns to identify inorganic bot activity.

Is my Spotify login required?

No. SpotCheck processes publicly available Spotify data directly in your browser. We never ask for your Spotify password or private account credentials.

Which browsers are supported?

SpotCheck supports Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, Arc, Brave, Microsoft Edge, and Opera.

Can I export validation reports?

Yes, Premium and Pro subscribers can export full validation breakdowns to CSV or JSON with a single click.
