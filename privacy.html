Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 24, 2026

Spot Check ("we", "our", or "us") is operated by Total Audio Promo Ltd. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with the Spot Check browser extension and web application located at spotcheck.cc.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

Spot Check is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do NOT:

  • Access, collect, or store your Spotify login credentials or passwords.
  • Access your private playlists, listening history, or saved library.
  • Track your general web browsing activity outside of public Spotify playlist pages.
  • Sell, rent, or trade any personal data to third parties.

2. Public Spotify Data Processed

When you validate a playlist, Spot Check retrieves publicly accessible metadata from Spotify's API, including:

  • Playlist name, cover image, and description.
  • Public follower count and track list count.
  • Public curator display name and profile URL.

This public information is evaluated locally in your browser to calculate bot risk scores and authenticity signals.

3. Premium Accounts & Payment Data

If you subscribe to Spot Check Premium or Pro, payment processing is handled securely by Stripe. We do not store credit card numbers on our servers. Your email address is stored solely for subscription management and authentication.

4. Analytics

We collect anonymized event analytics (e.g., check frequency and feature usage) to improve product performance. No personally identifiable music data is transmitted or sold.

Questions about Privacy?

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy, please contact us at info@totalaudiopromo.com.
